      Handbook rules — High School

      From employee handbook

      Benefit eligibility

      Periods / weekPayTuition & parsonagePTOYomtov & vacation
      1–8Hourly (paid when present)NoNoNo
      9–14Per period / salariedYesYesNo
      10–14, 10th year or morePer period / salariedYesYesYes
      15+Per period / salariedYesYesPaid

      Under 15 periods: paid only for time actually taught. End of May and June: employees must fully report during contracted hours or pay is prorated. PTO, Simcha and Maternity Leave apply only to eligible employees.

      PTO allowance

      • 1 week's worth of personal/sick days and lateness (e.g. 3 days/week = 3 days; 12 periods/week = 12 periods)
      • Starts once the teacher starts teaching; delayed starts are not covered
      • Leaves mid-year: reviewed at max 1 day per 2 months worked
      • Does not carry over to the next school year
      • Calculated automatically from the time clock; once used up, absences are deducted from pay

      Attendance & docking

      • Lateness is taken from PTO first, then deducted from pay
      • Missed punch counts as an absence until the principal corrects it
      • Day before/after a vacation or Yom Tov is double-docked
      • Extra personal days: double-docked, or paid only for days worked
      • Simcha, maternity and snow days must be marked excused by the attendance coordinator, or they count as unexcused

      Child's Lechaim1 day (day of or morning after)

      Shiva1 week

      A week = total scheduled hours in the employee's workweek. Paid by calendar days; a canceled class does not push the day off. The teacher must have the principal mark it as a simcha day and confirm.
